A Collection of Poems

Four humble pieces, written just for you.

Could I perhaps.. Have a moment?

From Afar

When I first met you.
I believed we would speak but few.
For my mind was ahaze with suspicion.
A state that only worsened my disposition.
So I decided to keep away.
If only to not lead us astray.

But once I discovered your love for art.
Watching from afar, I deemed the wiser part.
My interest grew when I learned you were a Vtuber too.
But the distance that kept me from speaking remained true.
Creepy as it may be, I continued to observe.
If only to burn into my eyes, a beauty well preserved.

Satisfied with the status quo I held in the past.
Perhaps that very thought was never meant to last.
I learned more of thee through words from friends.
And wished to know the soul that lay beyond such trends.
So I bided my time, to watch the beauty.
And donned my quiet mantle, treating it as duty.

Safe Yet Dangerous

Oh I only knew so little.
Of how your safety could be so brittle.
To watch you suffer alone, it brings so much pain.
If only I could approach and help to ease your strain.
You, who seemed so bright and strong,
Yet fueled by a world that continued to do you wrong.

The terror beneath my feet awakens to consume.
Still, in my mind, all I could do was assume.
The desire to help you, continues to grow.
If only my own doubts would let me show.
Truly I wanted nothing but to grow closer still.
But I knew the me then could only stand in the chill.

So I remained distant, yet close all the same.
If only to provide comfort without adding to their pain.
At the time I thought myself unworthy,
Someone deserving of nothing but rags that are dirty.
To wish for your safety I could only desire.
Yet still we lick wounds in the dangers of their ire.

Closer

I don't even remember how it began.
All I know is that one day we had a plan.
Plans that could only be described as completely dumb.
So much so that there were many moments where my face went numb.
Unsure if I was even allowed to laugh at first.
Yet as I learned more about you, I could not help but let the laughs burst.

How infectious your joy was, I thought.
That maybe I could observe further, so I sought.
Truly it went by like a blurry dream.
Like the tears that long since faded downstream.
From Yaoi to Anime, our joyous conversations went.
To the deepest parts of our souls, revealed whenever we vent..

Back then I still feared that you might grow exhausted.
But with patience so thick, you made me feel so accepted.
To trust someone fully, more than merely in name,
I could only hope then that you would feel the same.
So a took a leap then, to ask if we could hang out more.
In hindsight, you were the only one who held the key to the door.

I'd Like to Walk With You

Sometimes I find it difficult to find the words.
It's really difficult to speak in present verse.
Yet here I am speaking to you through this little thing.
Forcing my brain to think about how each word can fit like a ring.
So I will speak plain, simple and honest.
Just how you like it, with your ego so modest.

Your strength is amazing, your passion awe-inspiring,
Your will to pursue what you love despite the pain, never tiring.
Your presence is calming, your voice is soothing, and your art speaks more than words can say.
Through that strength, I've seen the hidden sides you keep away.
I don't try to shame you or berate you for doing so, however.
In truth, it only makes me want to care for you more than ever.

So if you'd allow me, whenever darkness grows,
Please let me be a light within your world of woes.
To care for you like a close friend.
To watch you grow and move forward until the end.
For better or worse, as long as you'd let me see it through.
Reyen, I'd like to walk with you.

Thank You For Being Alive

I am so happy to have met you. I wish to stay by your side from now on. To bear witness to your ever growing passion. To watch as you grow and grow, as an individual... And as a friend.

I hope you will allow me to stay by your side forevermore.

Happy 22nd Birthday, Reyen.♡
